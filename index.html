IEEE Conference Paper

Enhancing Explainable Diabetes Prediction with CTGAN Based Data Augmentation: A Comparative Study with SMOTE

2026 5th IEEE International Conference on Robotics, Automation, Artificial-intelligence and Internet-of-Things (RAAICON)

Abstract Summary:

Class imbalance presents a severe challenge in clinical machine learning models, often degrading sensitivity for high-risk diabetic patients. In this research, we propose a CTGAN (Conditional Tabular GAN) synthetic data augmentation workflow combined with SHAP/LIME Explainable AI frameworks to balance medical dataset distributions. Comparative evaluation against standard SMOTE demonstrates superior synthetic feature correlation alignment and improved diagnostic interpretability.

Methodology CTGAN Tabular Synthesis
Interpretability Explainable AI (XAI)
